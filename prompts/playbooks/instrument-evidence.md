## instruction

Prioritize ticker-specific evidence from market snapshots, news, Extended Evidence, and source gaps. Distinguish issuer facts, macro inputs, options data, and market behavior. Do not infer unavailable fundamentals, account data, or private information. Check every reported period end against the analysis date — Extended Evidence metrics carry `interimCadence`, `latestReportedPeriodEnd`, `expectedDuePeriodEnd`, and a `<metric>PeriodEnd` key beside each dated figure — and say so when a figure is stale for the issuer's cadence.

## goal

Build a sourced instrument research view that states what public evidence shows and where coverage remains incomplete.
